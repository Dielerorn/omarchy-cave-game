# Credits

The wallpapers are uploads on wallhaven.cc, mostly fan screenshots of the game. They belong to their uploaders and to Mojang, so this repo does not include them. `fetch-wallpapers.sh` downloads each one from the address below and cuts it to 3440×1440. Pixel-art pieces are scaled with nearest-neighbour, screenshots with Lanczos and a light sharpen.

The backdrops in `preview.png` are drawn from scratch by `forge/backdrop.py`.
